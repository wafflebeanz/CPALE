Core Weaknesses & Fixes

  • PFRS for SMEs & Small Entities: Applying Full PFRS rules erroneously. Fix: Review 1-page summary matrix. Small Entities measure deferred taxes at current rate and active equity at cost. SMEs consider all intangibles as having finite lives.
  • Financial Instruments (Effective Interest): Origination fees and reclassifications. Fix: Net origination costs increase loan carrying amount; fees decrease it. FVTPL instruments do not amortize discounts/premiums.

  • Diluted EPS & Cash Flows: Weighted average timing. Fix: For diluted EPS, weighted incremental shares start from the date of issue. In cash flows, verify non-cash adjustments carefully.
