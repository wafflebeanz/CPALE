PSA 700

📝 Introduction & General Purpose FS

General Purpose FS: Financial statements prepared in accordance with a general-purpose framework, designed to meet the common financial information needs of a wide range of users. Composed of a complete set of financial statements.

Audit Reporting Objectives:
Formation of Opinion (Based on evidence) + Expression of Opinion (Written report)
Basic Elements

📋 10 Basic Parts of the Auditor's Report

1. Title & 2. Addressee
  • Title: Distinguishes independent auditor's report. Highlights independence ("Independent Auditor's Report").
  • Addressee: Commonly to shareholders and/or TCWG (BOD). Should not only be to specific officer (management). If filed to SEC, must indicate complete mailing address.
3. Opinion Paragraph (Always First!)

Identifies entity, states FS have been audited, identifies each statement, specifies dates/periods. Precedes the Basis for Opinion.

Opinion Type Standard Wording / Phrase
Unmodified "Presents fairly, in all material respects" OR "Give a true and fair view"
Qualified "Except for the effects (or possible effects)..."
Adverse "Because of the significance... do not present fairly"
Disclaimer "We were engaged to audit..." AND "We do not express an opinion (or conclusion)..."
4. Basis for Opinion Paragraph
  • Always follows Opinion paragraph. States audit conducted per PSAs, refers to auditor's responsibilities section.
  • States independence per IESBA Code of Ethics.
  • States belief that audit evidence is sufficient and appropriate (EXCEPT for Disclaimer).
Disclaimer Specific Rule: Shall NOT include a reference to the section of auditor's responsibilities nor a statement that audit evidence obtained is sufficient and appropriate.
5. Management & 6. Auditor's Responsibilities

Management (and TCWG): Preparing FS, internal controls, assessing going concern, overseeing financial reporting.

Auditor's Responsibilities:

  • Objective: Reasonable Assurance (High level, not a guarantee).
  • Misstatements from fraud or error; materiality definition.
  • Exercise professional judgment & skepticism.
  • Location: Within body, in an appendix, or reference to a website (if legally permitted).
7-10. Partner, Signature, Address, Date & Supp. Info
  • Name of Partner: Required for Listed Entities. Exception: Significant personal security threat (Must discuss with TCWG).
  • Signature: Firm name, personal name, or both. For SEC: Must be personal name with PRC/BOA/TIN details.
  • Address: Jurisdiction location.
  • Date: Not earlier than FS Approval date OR date sufficient appropriate evidence obtained.
Supplementary Information Rules:
If Integral: Covered by Opinion.
If Not Integral: Must clearly differentiate. If FS gets Adverse opinion, auditor is Precluded from issuing a separate Unmodified opinion on the clearly differentiated supplementary info to avoid confusion.

💡 PSA 700 & 705 Drill Cards

PSA 701

🔑 Key Audit Matters (KAM)

  • Matters of most significance to the audit of the CURRENT period.
  • Selected from matters communicated with TCWG.
  • NOT a substitute for disclosure, modified opinion, or going concern uncertainty.
  • NOT applicable to Disclaimer of Opinion.
KAM Determination Process:
1. Communicated to TCWG ➔ 2. Required Significant Attention ➔ 3. Most Significant (KAM)

Exceptions: Precluded by law, or adverse consequences outweigh public interest, or matters requiring opinion modification.

PSA 706

📌 Emphasis of Matter (EOM) vs Other Matter (OM)

Emphasis of Matter (EOM) Other Matter (OM)
Matter appropriately presented/disclosed in FS, fundamental to user understanding. Matter OTHER THAN those presented/disclosed in FS, relevant to audit understanding.
Examples (MUSE):
- Major catastrophe
- Uncertainty of future exceptional litigation
- Significant subsequent event
- Early application of new accounting standard 		Examples (PrISM CoRe):
- Predecessor's Audit Opinion
- Inconsistency (Material) in Other Info
- Subsequent Discovery of Facts
- More than 1 set of FS
- Comparative Information
- Restriction on Distribution
PSA 710

🗓️ Comparative Information

Corresponding Figures vs Comparative FS
  • Corresponding Figures: Integral part of Current FS. Opinion refers to Current Period FS ONLY. (Do not specifically identify comparatives).
  • Comparative FS: Separate FS included for comparison. Opinion refers to EACH period presented.
Corresponding Figures - Modification Rules
Prior Modified, Unresolved: Modify opinion on Current Period FS.
Prior Modified, Resolved/Disclosed: Unmodified on Current (No reference to previous modified opinion). May add EOM if material.
Prior Misstated (Unmodified), Not Restated: Qualified or Adverse Opinion on Current.
Prior Misstated, Properly Restated in Current: Unmodified Opinion + EOM Paragraph.
Predecessor Auditor Rule: If referring to them, place in Other Matters Paragraph. Indicate prior audit, opinion type (and reasons if modified), and report date. Do NOT include Predecessor's Name.
PSA 720

📚 Other Information

Financial/non-financial info included in entity's annual report, outside FS and audit report. Auditor's job is to read for Material Inconsistency.

  • Listed Entities: Info obtained OR expects to obtain.
  • Non-Listed: Info actually obtained as of report date.
Effect on Auditor's Report:
Amend FS? Management Refuses ➔ Qualified or Adverse Opinion

Amend Other Info? Management Refuses ➔ Unmodified Opinion + Other Matter Paragraph

PSA 800 & 805

🎯 Special Purpose FS & Single Elements

PSA 800: Special Purpose FS

FS prepared using a special-purpose framework (e.g., Cash Basis, Tax Basis, Regulator Provisions, Contract Provisions).

MANDATORY REQUIREMENT:
Report SHALL include an Emphasis of Matter Paragraph
To alert users it may not be suitable for other purposes.

If FS are NOT Suitably Titled and Notes do not disclose framework ➔ Modified Opinion.

PSA 805: Single FS & Specific Elements

Materiality is usually LOWER than Complete Set (more extensive examination).

If Complete Set FS gets Adverse or Disclaimer:
Can you issue Unmodified on a Specific Element? ONLY IF: Not prohibited by law, published separately, AND is NOT a major portion.

Can you issue Unmodified on a Single FS? NEVER. It is always deemed a major portion.
PSA 810

📊 Summary Financial Statements

  • Must be THE SAME auditor who audited the Complete FS.
  • Opinion is on CONSISTENCY with complete FS, NOT "fair presentation".
  • Auditor issues ONLY Unmodified or Adverse Opinion.
  • Date cannot be earlier than Complete FS report date.
Condition Opinion on Summary FS
Inconsistent with Complete FS Adverse Opinion on Consistency
Consistent, but Complete FS has Qualified, EOM, or OM State Complete report contains it, describe basis and effects. Give opinion on Consistency.
Complete FS has Adverse or Disclaimer State it is Inappropriate to Express an Opinion on Summary FS.

PSA 230

📁 Audit Documentation (Working Papers)

Audit Documentation: Also known as working papers or work papers. It is the record of procedures performed, relevant audit evidence obtained, and conclusions reached.

Prepared in a way that enables an Experienced Auditor (one with practical audit experience and reasonable understanding of audit processes, PSAs, business environment, and reporting issues) with no previous connection to the audit to understand the nature, timing, extent, and results of procedures performed.

Primary Purpose: Provides principal support for the auditor's report/opinion AND the representation that the audit was performed per PSAs.
Crucial Rule: Does NOT provide support for the FS itself—the FS are supported by the client's accounting records and source documents.
Form, Content, and Extent & Inclusions vs. Exclusions
  • Must record: Who performed work, when (date), who reviewed it, date and extent of review.
  • Significant matters, discussions with management/TCWG (and when/whom), and significant judgments.
  • Should indicate that accounting records agree or reconcile with the FS.
  • Departures from relevant PSA requirements and alternative procedures (only in exceptional circumstances).

✔️ Usually Includes

  • Audit programs & Checklists
  • Memoranda & Analyses
  • Correspondence on significant matters
  • Confirmation & Representation letters

❌ Excludes

  • Client's accounting records (auditor only includes tracking numbers, not duplicates)
  • Superseded drafts of working papers and FS
  • Notes reflecting incomplete or preliminary thinking
  • Previous copies corrected for errors
Classification of Working Papers
Classification Description & Examples
Permanent File Items of continuing significance in succeeding periods.
Examples: Organization charts, internal control flowcharts, Articles of Incorporation/By-laws, Bond agreements, long-term contracts, Engagement letter, work done for non-current assets/liabilities and equity.
Current File For the current year or single period use only.
Examples: Audited FS, Audit report, Audit strategy/plan/program, lead schedules, working trial balance, adjusting/reclass entries, work done for current assets/liabilities and profit/loss, time & expense reports, staff names.
Correspondence File Communication letters with the client (Mgt Rep Letter, Mgt Letter, Eng Letter). Can be integrated with Current File.
Tax File Copies of client's tax returns and important tax computations.
Preparation, Ownership, Assembly, and Retention
  • Preparation: Prepared by auditors. Clients should NOT assist in preparing working papers. Uses Headings, Indexing, Cross-Referencing (for supervision trail), and Tick Marks (symbols for procedures).
  • Ownership & Confidentiality: Owned by the auditor. Confidential, subject to privileged communication rule. Third party cannot access without client consent.
  • Assembly: Not later than 60 days after the date of the auditor's report. Includes sorting, collating, discarding superseded docs, signing checklists. No new audit procedures or conclusions are allowed during assembly.
  • Retention: For a period of at least 10 years from completion.
Revisions After Auditor's Report Date:
If due to new information, document when, by whom, reasons, and effect on conclusion. No deletion is allowed. Additions only if necessary.
PSA 260

🗣️ Communication with TCWG

Those Charged with Governance (TCWG) Management
Oversight function of the entity's strategic direction Conduct of entity's operations (Day-to-day)
Approves the FS (usual case) Prepares the FS (usual case)

Audit Committee: Part of TCWG who are not active in day-to-day management.

Matters to be Communicated
  • Auditor's Responsibilities: Forming/expressing opinion. Does NOT relieve management/TCWG of their responsibilities. Auditors do not design procedures specifically to identify governance matters. (Does NOT include amount of audit fees).
  • Planned Scope and Timing of the Audit
  • Significant Findings: Difficulties during audit, significant deficiencies, qualitative aspects of accounting policies/estimates, written representations requested, other significant matters.
  • Auditor Independence: Strictly required for Listed Entities.
Communication Process & Strict Rules
  • Form: Oral or Writing. MUST be in writing if PSAs require it, or if auditor judges oral communication to be inadequate.
  • Timing: Timely basis, not necessarily required to occur before the auditor's report is issued.
  • Not for Management: Not all matters communicated with TCWG need to also be communicated with management. Done to minimize expectations gap.
  • Adjustments: ALL significant adjustments proposed by the auditor must be communicated to TCWG, whether or not corrected by management.
  • Material Weaknesses: All material weaknesses in internal control must be reported to TCWG. However, the auditor is NOT required to recommend corrections.
🚫 DO NOT COMMUNICATE:
Materiality thresholds, tolerable deviation rates, and other specific detailed procedures are NOT communicated to TCWG.
Documentation Specifics (PSA 260):
Oral Document when and to whom it was communicated.

Written Retain a copy of the communication as part of the audit documentation.

PSRE 2400

🔎 Review Engagements

An assurance engagement providing a moderate level of assurance, expressed in a negative form.

Audit Procedures & Limitations
  • Planning: Similar to audit; must obtain understanding of business, industry, accounting systems, and operating characteristics.
  • Procedures are limited ONLY to:
    • Inquiry: Accounting principles, recording procedures, actions at meetings, inquiries to persons having responsibility for financial/accounting matters.
    • Analytical Procedures: Against budgets, prior year data, or industry average. (NOT against supporting documents/inspection).
  • Internal controls are NOT examined.
The Review Report & Conclusions

States that the review is conducted in accordance with PSREs (not PSAs) and that procedures are substantially less than an audit.

⚠️ Strict Rule: The report DOES NOT include a statement in a sense that goes "A review provides only a limited or negative assurance that the FS are presented fairly." (Because the conclusion itself is already expressed in a negative form).
Conclusion Type Standard Wording / Phrase
Unmodified "Nothing has come to our attention that causes us to believe..."
Qualified "Except for the effects... nothing has come to our attention..."
Adverse "Because of the significance... do not present fairly..."
Disclaimer "We do not express a conclusion... unable to obtain sufficient appropriate evidence..."

Note: If the conclusion is modified, the "Basis for Conclusion" paragraph is placed before (preceding) the Conclusion Paragraph.

PSRS 4400 & 4410

🤝 Agreed-Upon Procedures & Compilation

PSRS 4400: Agreed-Upon

  • Non-assurance: No opinion, no assurance. Independence not required.
  • Users draw their own conclusions from the auditor's work.
  • Report is Restricted to users who agreed to the procedures (unaware persons may misinterpret results).
  • Report on Factual Findings: Must specifically list procedures agreed upon and factual findings/errors for each.
  • Must state: "Procedures performed do not constitute an audit nor a review."

PSRS 4410: Compilation

  • Non-assurance: Uses accounting/financial reporting expertise (not auditing). Independence not required.
  • Must obtain acknowledgment from management/TCWG that they take responsibility for the final version.
  • Report Rules: Explains practitioner is not required to verify accuracy/completeness. Does not express audit/review opinion.
  • Mandatory: Most likely to include in each page of the Compiled FI/FS an indication that no assurance is provided.
  • May omit note disclosures.
Compilation Withdrawal Triggers:
Practitioner must read the compiled info and propose amendments if:
1. It doesn't describe the reporting framework.
2. It is materially misstated.
3. It is misleading.
➔ If management refuses to amend, OR fails to provide records: The practitioner must Withdraw from the engagement and inform management/TCWG.
PSAE 3400

🔮 Prospective Financial Information (PFI)

An Assurance Engagement based on assumptions about events that may occur in the future and possible actions by an entity.

PFI Assurance Breakdown:
Moderate Assurance (Negative) on Reasonableness of Assumptions
+
Positive Assurance (Opinion) on Preparation & Presentation

Does NOT express an opinion as to whether the results shown in the PFI will actually be achieved!

Forecast vs. Projection
Forecast Projection
Prepared on best-estimate assumptions (expected to happen). Prepared on hypothetical assumptions (not necessarily expected to take place).
Predicts what is expected to happen. Predicts what may happen depending on scenarios.
May either be for general or limited use. For limited use only.
More associated to short-term; conservative. More associated to long-term; less conservative.
Management Representations & Modified Opinions

Written representations required for: Intended use of PFI, completeness of significant assumptions, and management's acceptance of responsibility.

Condition / Issue Effect on PFI Report
Presentation and disclosure of PFI is not adequate Qualified or Adverse Opinion (or Withdraw)
Examination is affected by conditions precluding necessary procedures Disclaimer of Opinion (or Withdraw)
Significant assumptions do not provide reasonable basis Adverse Opinion (or Withdraw)
Assumptions are clearly unrealistic / inappropriate for use Do not accept the engagement (or Withdraw)

PSQM 1

🌟 System of Quality Management (SQM)

Designed, implemented, and operated by a FIRM for all engagements under AASC (all services rendered by those in public practice). The objective is to provide the firm with reasonable assurance that it maintains compliance with standards and issues appropriate reports.

  • Operates in a continual and iterative manner (responsive to changes, not linear).
  • Always complied by the firm unless not relevant due to the nature and circumstances.
  • Quality management is implemented BOTH at the firm level and on individual engagements.
  • Fun Fact: The least important element in evaluating a CPA firm's SQM is the policies with respect to the determination of audit fees.
  • Effective Date: December 15, 2022.

8 Components

🧩 The 8 Components of PSQM 1

I. Risk Assessment & II. Governance and Leadership

I. Risk Assessment Process

Establishes quality objectives, identifies/assesses quality risks (reasonable possibility of occurring that adversely affects objectives), and designs/implements responses.

II. Governance and Leadership (Mnemonics: CARABAO)

  • Culture (demonstrates commitment to quality)
  • Accountability and Responsibility
  • Actions and Behaviors
  • Allocation of resources
  • Organizational structure
Ultimate Responsibility: Firm's CEO, Managing Partners, or Board. Evaluates SQM AT LEAST ANNUALLY.
Operational Responsibility: A partner with appropriate influence and authority.
III. Ethical Requirements & IV. Client Acceptance

III. Relevant Ethical Requirements

Fulfillment of responsibilities per the Code, including International Independence Standards. Independence must be confirmed AT LEAST ANNUALLY.

IV. Client Acceptance and Continuance

Judgments are based on the nature/circumstances, client integrity, and firm's ability (competence/resources).

Strict Rule: No inappropriate judgments about client acceptance/continuance are allowed due to Financial Priorities (profitability, fees) or Operational Priorities (market share growth, industry specialization). SQM should not be compromised.
V. Engagement Performance ("JUSCO Doc!")
  • Judgement (and professional skepticism).
  • Understands and fulfills responsibilities.
  • Supervision, Direction, and Review (SDR).
  • Consultations on difficult/contentious matters.
  • Opinions (Differences in Opinion are resolved and documented).
  • Doc! (Engagement Documentation).
Direction Instructions before the start of the task.
Supervision Tracking ongoing task. The tool used for supervision is the Audit Program.
Review Critical recheck of completed task as to adequacy of documentation.
Engagement Documentation Rule: Assembled on a timely basis within 60 days after report date, and retained for at least 10 years.
VI. Resources & VII. Information and Communication

VI. Resources (HITS)

Includes Human, Intellectual (methodologies, guides), Technological (IT apps), and Service Providers. The firm remains responsible for its SQM even if using service providers. Excludes external experts and internal auditors providing direct assistance.

VII. Information and Communication

Information system generates/maintains reliable info. Culture reinforces exchange of info within the firm and with external parties (TCWG, Network Firms, Regulators).

VIII. Monitoring/Remediation & IX. Documentation

VIII. Monitoring and Remediation Process

Enables SQM evaluation (ongoing/periodic at least annually). Evaluates severity of deficiencies and performs root cause analysis.

Findings Information indicating that deficiencies may exist.

Deficiency Exists when an objective is not established, a risk is not identified/addressed, or a response does not reduce risk to an acceptably low level.

IX. Documentation (of the SQM itself)

Provides evidence of design, implementation, and operation.

Firm's SQM Retention Rule:
Established as a matter of professional judgment sufficient to enable monitoring.
(IT IS NOT STRICTLY 10 YEARS, unlike engagement working papers!)
PSQM 2 & PSA 220

PSQM 2: EQR

  • Objective evaluation of significant judgments.
  • EQR'er Eligibility: May be a partner, other individual, or external. MUST NOT be an engagement team member.

Concerns:
Present & Unresolved: Notify firm EQR cannot be completed.
Absent: Notify engagement partner EQR is complete.

RA 9298

⚖️ Philippine Accountancy Act of 2004

Objectives of RA 9298

  1. Standardization and regulation of accounting education.
  2. Examination and registration of CPAs.
  3. Supervision, control, and regulation of the practice of accountancy in the Philippines.

Enforcement

  • Primary duty of the PRC and the BOA.
  • Violations filed with appropriate charges by PRC, assisted by BOA.
  • Secretary of Justice (or representative) acts as legal adviser and renders legal assistance.
Punishment for Violation (RA 9298 & IRR):
Fine: Not less than ₱50,000
Imprisonment: Not exceeding 2 years
(Either or both, at the discretion of the court)

📝 CPA Licensure Examination

Qualifications & Requirements

Qualifications to Take:

  • Filipino citizen (natural-born or naturalized)
  • Of good moral character
  • Holder of BSA Degree
  • Not convicted of any criminal offense involving moral turpitude

Requirements to Apply:

  • Birth certificate (or proof of naturalization)
  • Transcript of records (TOR)
  • NBI clearance

Results must be submitted by BOA to PRC within 10 calendar days, unless extended for just cause.

Status Conditions
PASSED Average of at least 75% AND No grades below 65% in any subject.
CONDITIONAL Didn't meet both passing qualifications, but got at least 75% in a majority (4) of the subjects.
Given 2 years for the removal exam (counted as one exam with the original).
FAILED Did not meet both passed or conditional qualifications.
Refresher Course Rule
Trigger Required upon 2 complete failures.

Requirements At least 24 units from a BOA-accredited school (Not a review center). Valid for 2 years or 2 failed takes, whichever comes first.
