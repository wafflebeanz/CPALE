1. Deposits in Transit (DIT) Roll-Forward

RECEIPTS ENGINE

Cash receipts debited on entity's books in one month, but not yet credited by the bank until the subsequent month.

[INVENTORY LOGIC] DIT, Beg + Pure Book Receipts − Pure Bank Credits = DIT, End
★ Solving Beginning: DIT, Beg = DIT, End − Pure Book Receipts + Pure Bank Credits

• Pure Book Receipts: Total book debits to Cash minus non-deposit credits (e.g., prior-month CM recorded late, uncorrected book receipt overstatements).

• Pure Bank Credits: Total bank statement credits minus direct bank collections (current-month unrecorded CMs, bank error correction credits).

2. Outstanding Checks (OC) Roll-Forward

DISBURSEMENT ENGINE

Checks issued and credited by the entity, but not yet cleared/debited by the bank at the reconciliation date.

[INVENTORY LOGIC] OC, Beg + Pure Book Disb. − Pure Bank Checks Cleared = OC, End
★ Squeeze Formula: OC, End = Cleared Beg Checks + Book Disb. − Bank Debits Cleared

• Pure Book Disbursements: Cash credits minus non-check bank debits (e.g., prior-month bank service charges recorded this month).

• Pure Bank Checks Cleared: Bank charges minus debit memos (service charges, bounced/NSF customer checks, direct bank debits).

3. Proof of Cash: Bank-to-Book Columns

RECONCILING SIGNS
  • Receipts Column: Bank Credits − DIT, Beg + DIT, End + Prior CM − Current CM = Book Receipts.
  • Disbursements Column: Bank Debits − OC, Beg + OC, End − Prior DM + Current DM = Book Disbursements.
  • Rule for Squeeze: If prior OC/DIT is unknown, isolate the cleared portion that belonged to the prior period.

4. Calculation Fallacies & Exam Traps

EXAM TRAPS
TRAP 1: The "Direct Deduction" Illusion Subtracting unrecorded Current CM (₱800k) directly from Ending DIT (₱1.5M) is a mathematical coincidence. It only worked because net adjustments (7.8M − 7.0M) happened to equal ₱800k. Never subtract CM directly from DIT!
TRAP 2: Redeposited NSF Checks with "No Entry" If an NSF check bounces and is redeposited without book entries, it is neither in book receipts nor book disbursements. Do not adjust the books for it!
TRAP 3: Certified Checks Exclusion Certified checks are immediately deducted from the depositor's account by the bank upon certification. Never include certified checks in Outstanding Checks!
TRAP 4: Prior DM/CM Recorded in Current Month Recorded late by the entity. They reside inside current book cash records, but represent last month's bank activity. They must be stripped out of current-month movements.

5. The "Who Knows It First" Principle

AUDIT CORE
GOLDEN RULE: Timing differences exist because one party recorded a transaction while the other party is still unaware or delayed.

• Book Recorded First: DIT (Receipts) & OC (Disbursements) → Adjusted on Bank Balance.

• Bank Recorded First: Credit Memos (Receipts) & Debit Memos (Disbursements) → Adjusted on Book Balance.
