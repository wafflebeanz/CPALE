✨ Downstream: Allocate to Parent only ✨ Upstream: Allocate to Parent & Sub

🌸 1. Assets & Liabilities

💰

Consolidated Total Assets

  • + Assets - Parent BV
  • + Assets - Subsidiary FV
  • + Purchase Price (in form of assets)
  • + Goodwill
  • - Direct / Indirect Cost (deduct if paid)
  • - CTIR - stocks
  • - CTIR - debt
  • = Conso Total Assets
🧾

Consolidated Total Liabilities

  • + Liab - Parent BV
  • + Liab - Subsidiary FV
  • + Purchase Price (in form of debt)
  • + Contingent Liab
  • + DCAC
  • - CTIR - stocks (deduct if paid)
  • - CTIR - debt (deduct if paid)
  • = Conso Total Liability

🎀 2. Shareholders' Equity (SHE)

Consolidated Total SHE

    Method 1 (Components)
  • + SHE - Parent
  • + NCI
  • + PP (form of shares)
  • + Gain/Loss (G/L on Remeas, Provision, etc.)
  • - DC (Direct Costs)
  • - IC (Indirect Costs)
  • - CTIR - stocks
  • = Conso Total SHE
    Method 2 (Combined)
  • + SHE - parent, beg
  • + NCI, beg
  • + Conso Net Income
  • + PP (form of shares) @FV
  • - CTIR - stocks
  • - Dividend Declared - Parent
  • - Dividend Received by NCI fr Sub
  • = Conso Total SHE

Share Capital & Premium

  • 🔹 Share Capital - Parent
  • 🔹 + PP (shares @ par)
  • ✨ = Conso Share Capital
  • 🔸 Share Premium - Parent
  • 🔸 + PP (excess of FV over par)
  • 🔸 - CTIR - stocks
  • ✨ = Conso Share Premium

Retained Earnings (RE)

  • + Conso RE, beg (or RE-parent on acq date)
  • + Conso NI - parent
  • - Dividends Declared - parent
  • = Conso RE, End
🍰

☁️ 3. Assets (PPE & Inventory) & COS

🏭

Consolidated PPE

  • PPE - Parent BV +
  • PPE - Subsidiary BV +
  • UVA of PPE +
  • Amort of PPE (UVA) -
  • OVA of PPE -
  • Amort of OVA +
  • = Conso PPE
📦
📦

Consolidated Inventory

  • Inventory - Parent +
  • Inventory - Sub BV +
  • UVA of Invty +
  • Amort of UVA -
  • OVA of Invty -
  • Amort of OVA +
  • Unrealized Intercompany Profit (End Invty) -
  • = Conso Inventory
⚠️ Note: Amortization of inventory is through sale
🛒

Consolidated Cost of Sales

  • COS - Parent +
  • COS - Subsidiary +
  • Invty Amortization (UVA/OVA) +/-
  • = Conso Cost of Sales

Consolidated OPEX 📉

  • OPEX - Parent +
  • OPEX - Subsidiary +
  • Amortization UVA-PPE + through depreciation
  • Amortization OVA-PPE -
  • Impairment Loss - Goodwill +
= Conso OPEX
*GIRP: Gain on rem of phi, gain on rem of provision/contingent beyond mp

Consolidated Gain on Sale 🎉

  • Gain on Sale - Parent +
  • Gain on Sale - Subsidiary +
  • Amort of UVA-PPE - through sale
  • Amort of OVA-PPE +
= Conso Gain on Sale

Consolidated Loss on Sale 😢

  • Loss on Sale - Parent +
  • Loss on Sale - Subsidiary +
  • Amort of UVA-PPE + through sale
  • Amort of OVA-PPE -
= Conso Loss On Sale

🐻 5. Goodwill Impairment & NCI

📊

Non-Controlling Interest (NCI)

  • NCI, beg +
  • Conso NI - NCI +
  • Div Received by NCI from Sub -
  • = NCI, end

📝 NCI Note:

If 1st year: use IM of NCI.
If subsequent years: use NCI end of last yr.
