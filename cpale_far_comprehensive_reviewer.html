Cash & Receivables

  • Cash equivalents: Short-term, highly liquid, insignificant value risk. The 3-month rule is measured from the date of acquisition, not reporting date.
  • Bank overdrafts: Included only if repayable on demand and part of cash management (IAS 7).
  • Trade receivables: Initially measured at transaction price if no significant financing component.
  • Long-term notes: Measured at present value when financing effect is material.

Debt vs. Equity Investments

  • Transaction costs: Added to initial carrying amount unless FVPL (expensed).
  • Equity FVOCI: Irrevocable election for non-trading equity. Cumulative OCI is never recycled to P&L. Dividends go to P&L if return on investment. No ECL applies.
  • Debt FVOCI: ECL applies. Cumulative OCI is recycled to P&L upon derecognition.

Expected Credit Losses (ECL)

  • Stage 1: No significant credit risk increase. Allowance = 12-month ECL. Interest based on gross carrying amount.
  • Stage 2: Significant increase in credit risk. Allowance = Lifetime ECL. Interest based on gross carrying amount.
  • Stage 3: Credit-impaired. Allowance = Lifetime ECL. Interest based on net carrying amount.
Key Formulas Interest Income = Opening Gross Carrying Amount * EIR
Discount Amortization = Interest Income - Cash Interest
Premium Amortization = Cash Interest - Interest Income
CPALE Trap: Passing SPPI does not automatically mean amortised cost. The business model must also be satisfied. 12-month ECL is not the cash shortfall expected in the next 12 months; it reflects lifetime cash shortfalls from defaults possible within the next 12 months.

Inventory & PPE

  • NRV (Net Realizable Value): Est. selling price - est. completion costs - est. selling costs.
  • LIFO is prohibited. Use FIFO, weighted average, or specific identification.
  • PPE Initial Cost: Includes purchase price (net of discounts), directly attributable costs, and PV of dismantling obligations.
  • Depreciation: Begins when the asset is available for use, not when actually used. Land improvements have finite lives.

Intangibles & Agriculture

  • Research: Always expensed.
  • Development: Capitalized ONLY after technical feasibility, intention, ability to use/sell, future benefits, resources, and reliable measurement are demonstrated.
  • Internally generated brands/customer lists: Never capitalized.
  • Agriculture: Bearer plants = IAS 16 (PPE). Produce on plant = IAS 41. Harvested produce = IAS 2 (Inventory).

Revaluation & Investment Property

  • IAS 16 Revaluation: Upward revaluation generally goes to OCI (Revaluation Surplus). Not recycled to P&L upon derecognition.
  • IAS 40 Fair Value Model: Fair value changes go directly to Profit or Loss.

Impairment (IAS 36)

Impairment Workflow [1] Calculate Recoverable Amount = HIGHER of Value in Use (VIU) and Fair Value Less Costs of Disposal (FVLCD).
[2] Compare to Carrying Amount (CA). If CA > Recoverable Amount, recognize loss.
[3] Allocate to CGU: First to Goodwill, then pro-rata to other assets.
  • Goodwill impairment: Never reversed.
CPALE Trap: NRV is entity-specific and NOT the same as fair value less costs to sell. Recoverable amount is the HIGHER, not the lower, of VIU and FVLCD. For IFRS 5, management intention alone is insufficient; criteria must be met.

Financial Liabilities & Compound Instruments

  • Transaction costs: Deducted from initial carrying amount.
  • Compound Instruments (Convertible Bonds): Measure liability first (using market rate for similar nonconvertible debt). Equity is the residual.
Lease Computation (Lessee) [1] Lease Liability = PV of unpaid lease payments (use implicit rate or IBR).
[2] ROU Asset = Initial Liability + Prepays/Incentives + Initial Direct Costs + Restoration.
[3] Subsequent: Interest on liability + Depreciation on ROU asset.

Provisions & Contingencies

  • Provision: Recognize (Probable outflow).
  • Contingent Liability: Disclose unless remote (Possible outflow).
  • Contingent Asset: Disclose if probable; recognize ONLY if virtually certain.
  • Onerous Contract: Provision based on unavoidable costs of meeting obligations.

Current vs. Non-Current

  • Focuses on rights existing at the reporting date.
  • Right to defer settlement for at least 12 months must exist at reporting date. Post-reporting date refinancing does not count.

Income Taxes & Equity

  • Permanent Differences: Affect current tax, NO deferred tax.
  • Taxable Temporary Differences: Create DTLs.
  • Deductible Temporary Differences: Create DTAs.
  • Treasury Shares: Deducted from equity. NO gain/loss recognized in P&L on purchase, sale, issue, or cancellation.
  • Defined Benefits: Remeasurements (actuarial gains/losses) go to OCI and are not recycled.
CPALE Trap: Do not apply the coupon rate to the carrying amount for expense. Coupon = cash interest; EIR = interest expense. Current tax asks "what is taxable now?", Deferred tax asks "what future tax consequence is embedded?". Do not report treasury share 'gains' in P&L.
IFRS 15: Revenue from Contracts with Customers [1] Identify Contract --> [2] Identify Performance Obligations --> [3] Determine Transaction Price --> [4] Allocate Price (Relative SSP) --> [5] Recognize Revenue

Revenue & Returns

  • Allocation: Generally use relative stand-alone selling prices (SSP).
  • Timing: Recognize over time ONLY if criteria met; otherwise, point in time.
  • Variable Consideration: Constrained to avoid significant reversal.
  • Expected Returns: Require a refund liability and recovery asset.

Earnings Per Share (IAS 33)

  • Basic EPS: Profit attributable to ordinary equity holders / Weighted-average ordinary shares outstanding.
  • Treasury Shares: Excluded while held.
  • Bonus Issues/Splits: Require retrospective adjustment of share figures.
  • Diluted EPS: Potential ordinary shares included ONLY when dilutive (reduce EPS or increase loss per share).
EPS Computation Flow Compute the weighted-average denominator FIRST. Then deal with bonus/split effects, and finally calculate dilution impact.

Statement of Cash Flows (IAS 7)

  • Classification: Operating, Investing, Financing.
  • Noncash Transactions: Excluded from statement but disclosed (e.g., equipment acquired by issuing shares).

Policies, Estimates & Events (IAS 8 & 10)

  • Accounting Policy Change: Retrospective.
  • Accounting Estimate Change: Prospective. (e.g., useful life or depreciation method change).
  • Prior-Period Error: Retrospective restatement.
  • IAS 10 Adjusting Events: Evidence of conditions existing at reporting date (e.g., customer bankruptcy confirming year-end difficulty).
  • IAS 10 Non-Adjusting Events: Conditions arising after reporting date (e.g., fire, dividends declared post year-end).
CPALE Trap: Cash received is not automatically revenue; revenue follows satisfaction of obligations. A transaction can be investing/financing in nature but excluded from cash flow statement if no cash moved. Dividends declared after reporting date are NOT liabilities at reporting date. FINAL EXAM RULE: Identify the standard first, compute second.
